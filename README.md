# Multi-frame analysis improves close-pair detection in single-molecule TIRF microscopy

This repository holds the supplemental material of the paper

> Aref Enayati, Mathias S. Weyland, Marco Lattuada, Alke Petri-Fink, Oliver
> Castell, Rudolf Marcel Füchslin. Multi-frame analysis improves close-pair
> detection in single-molecule TIRF microscopy. Accepted for publication in the
> proceedings of WIVACE 2026 (Springer).

## Contents

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Supplemental_Material.pdf` | Supplemental Notes 1 to 7, Figures S1 to S5 and Tables S1 to S3, which the paper cites by number |

The seven notes are:

1. Simulated Data
2. Detector Architecture and Training
3. Decoding, Matching and Metrics
4. Close-Pair Injection
5. Reading the Close-Pair Decomposition
6. Replicate Training Runs
7. Training Curves

## Data

All results in the paper come from simulated videos. The videos and the
simulator that produced them come from our earlier study:

> Aref Enayati, Hannah M. Baird, Mathias S. Weyland, Alke Petri-Fink, Marco
> Lattuada, W. David Jamieson, Rudolf Marcel Füchslin, Oliver Castell. Enhanced
> Quantitative Accuracy in Single Molecule Imaging: A Fidelity-Guided Denoising
> Approach. Biophysical Journal (2026).
> https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2026.10.023

The data of that study are archived on Zenodo
(https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21925651), and its code is at
https://github.com/Bio-Hhost/Fidelity_Guided_Denoising_Approach.
